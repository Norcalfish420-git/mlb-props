Game Script

Colorado State (2-3, 0-1 Pac-12) is at Texas State (2-3, 0-1 Pac-12). First meeting. Both are in their first Pac-12 season. Texas State is home on a Thursday. Jim Mora is in his first year at Colorado State. GJ Kinne is in his fourth at Texas State. The line opened Texas State -11.5 and moved to -14.5. The total is 69.5.

What the writers are saying: numberFire, on FanDuel Research, has Texas State winning 85.8 percent of the time, at -14.5 with a total of 69.5. A matchup write at ats.io put the score at Texas State 41, Colorado State 24. Covers readers are 60 percent on Texas State and 63 percent on the over. Pete Thamel of ESPN wrote that Hauss Hejny will not start, and that K'saan Farrar is expected to start. Kevin Lytle of the Fort Collins Coloradoan quoted Jim Mora saying Hejny is practicing, not in a full role, and will be suited up, and that receiver RJ Vick is probably doubtful. The later injury report listed Vick questionable.

Where the defense is weaker

Texas State allows 300.4 passing yards a game (138th) and 122.8 rushing (44th). They just lost 31-29 at San Diego State while gaining 565 yards. The pass defense is the soft side.

Colorado State allows 493.2 yards a game (137th), 264.6 passing (128th) and 228.6 rushing (136th), and 38.6 points (133rd). Oregon State scored 56 on them on Oct. 3. They have allowed 59 and 56 in the two games before the bye.

What the scoreboard forces

ats.io wrote Texas State 41, Colorado State 24. That is a Texas State win inside the 69.5 total, with Colorado State still getting to the mid-20s.

Who that points at

Captain Torrance Burgess Jr. The books have him around 109.5 rushing yards, and Davian Jackson is out. Brad Jackson stays on the card. He throws and he runs. K'saan Farrar is the Colorado State quarterback who will start. Beau Sparks is the Texas State receiver. Tommy Maher is the Colorado State catcher who fits with Farrar while Vick is questionable. Sit Hauss Hejny, Gavin Parkhurst, Davian Jackson, and RJ Vick.
