Game Script

UCLA is a two-score home favorite at the Rose Bowl, late, outdoor. Bob Chesney’s first Big Ten game — he just took James Madison to the playoff, then brought Dean Kennedy (offense) and Colin Hitschler (defense) west with him. The books have UCLA about 14 points better and both teams around 52 points together. It is 71 degrees, wind at 5, 4% rain — fine for football. This is a two-score favorite, not a three-score stack.

What the writers are saying: UCLA wins. The number may be fat. One desk takes Purdue plus two scores because Iamaleava has not thrown a touchdown and all of UCLA’s scores have been on the ground. Another says the Bruins are better at home but this can stay tight down the stretch. The local paper says Purdue cannot set the edge against Knight and Iamaleava. We are not captaining a backup quarterback. We are not playing Towns.

UCLA wants to run. Last week they were run-heavy (43 rushes, 23 throws). Wayne Knight is the 5-foot-7 speedster Chesney stole from James Madison — nearly 1,400 yards there last year, and he already has 168 yards and six rushing scores in two games here. Anthony Woods is the other halfback (148 yards, 9.3 a carry). Iamaleava runs with them and has still not thrown a touchdown pass: 335 yards, two interceptions, zero scores through the air against Cal and San Diego State. Mikey Matthews is out; Semaj Morgan filled the slot last week (6 for 73). Landon Ellis is returning. Brayden Loftin is the tight end with a posted number — keep him over Towns.

Purdue wants to throw. Barry Odom is in year two after a 2–10. Ryan Browne has been phenomenal — 646 yards (14th in the country), four scores, no interceptions. Fame Ijeboi is the Minnesota-transfer back (over 200 yards and four scores). Asaad Waseem is the slot you keep with Browne — the FAU transfer leading a loaded receiver room (12 for 172 and a score). They rolled Indiana State, then lost 38–36 in overtime to Wake Forest, and they have averaged about 40 points. This is by far the hardest defense they have seen.

Where the defense is weaker

  • UCLA: Red-zone defense has allowed scores. New leadership under Hitschler. Iamaleava’s empty pass game is the real limit on a two-score script.
  • Purdue: Kevin Kane is back as coordinator. The front has not set the edge against running quarterbacks — Indiana State and Wake Forest both ran. Saturday is the first real test of whether that unit is fixed.

What the scoreboard forces

  • UCLA is favored by two scores at home, and the books have both teams around 52 points together. UCLA can sit on a lead.
  • If UCLA gets up by two scores, they run more. Wayne Knight gets the work, and Ryan Browne has to throw from behind.
  • If it stays within a score, Browne keeps throwing and Iamaleava has to throw too — he has not done that well.
  • If Purdue takes the lead, Iamaleava has to throw. He does not sit on the ball.

Who that points at

  • Captain Iamaleava or Knight. Iamaleava is the home pick because he throws and runs. Knight is the other captain if UCLA’s run game keeps rolling.
  • If Iamaleava is on the six, keep Semaj Morgan and Brayden Loftin with him.
  • Play Ijeboi. If Browne is on the six, keep a Purdue catcher with him.
  • Sit the backup quarterbacks. Sit Matthews. Sit Towns.

WHO THEY ARE

  • Purdue is unranked this year (1-1).
  • UCLA is unranked this year, unbeaten (2-0).
