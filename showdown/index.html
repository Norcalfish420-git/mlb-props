How this game plays

  • Home favorite about a touchdown and a half. Not a blowout number
  • The books have both teams around 51.5 together
  • Pitt already showed it can win ugly (12–7)

How they want to play

  • Pitt offense: Heintschel throws. The run is a committee. Narduzzi’s group wants to stop the run and keep the ball clean
  • Pitt defense: Held UCF to 20 rush yards. Brown called them a team that flies to the football
  • Syracuse offense: Angeli throws. The run game vs this front is thin, especially if Hill sits

Where the defense is weaker

  • Pitt: Week 2 offense was the hole (2.4 yards per carry, no passing scores). Writers are not naming a leaky Pitt front
  • Syracuse: Pass defense was a 2025 problem. This year’s Cal tape was better. Do not invent a hole from last year

What the scoreboard forces

  • A two-score Pitt lead is the grind — Ferguson/Turner, not more Heintschel deep shots

  • A Syracuse lead asks Heintschel to throw more than the UCF rain script

Who that points at
