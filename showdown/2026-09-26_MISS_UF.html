CFB Showdown — 2026-09-26 · Ole Miss @ Florida

2) Ole Miss @ Florida · 3:30 p.m. ET · Ben Hill Griffin Stadium · ABC

This slate is five out of five stars. No. 4 Ole Miss is a small road dog at No. 21 Florida. The live book is Florida −3.5 / 58.5. 3:30 p.m. ET on ABC. Outdoor. The Swamp.

  • Florida -3.5.
  • Total 58.5.
  • Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, afternoon.

Game Script

No. 4 Ole Miss (3-0) visits No. 21 Florida (3-0). Pete Golding is in his first year as the Ole Miss coach after he was the defensive coordinator. Jon Sumrall is in his first year at Florida after Tulane. The books have Florida around 31 and Ole Miss around 28. Both teams have already played someone. Ole Miss beat Louisville and LSU. Florida's real win was at Auburn, after earlier games against Florida Atlantic and Campbell. Trinidad Chambliss has completed about 70 percent for 924 yards, seven touchdowns and two interceptions. Against LSU he threw for 363. Aaron Philo has completed 73.4 percent for 721 yards, six touchdowns and two interceptions, plus two rushing scores. Jadan Baugh has 54 carries for 458 yards and eight touchdowns. Kewan Lacy has 39 carries for 191 and four scores, and the DraftKings report lists him doubtful.

What the writers are saying: The home paper calls it a shootout. Florida is top five in yards and first in points per possession, and both teams have already scored more than 110 points. The line opened Florida -1.5 and moved to -3.5. Jon Sumrall said he could not believe his team was favored. One Gainesville pick is Florida 41-38 if Jadan Baugh and Duke Clark shorten the game. Another is Ole Miss 38-34 because Florida has not played clean. The Ole Miss staff is mostly on the Rebels in a one-score game, 31-28 or 27-24, with one writer on Florida 34-31 if Kewan Lacy sits. A national preview takes the over because both offenses have scored on every red-zone trip and Auburn scored on six of 11 drives against this defense. We are not stacking a blowout.

Ole Miss is in its first true road game, between the LSU win and a bye, in a stadium that has sold out 21 straight. They led LSU 24-7 at half. Lacy had 43 yards and a score on nine carries in the first quarter, then landed on the shoulder he had surgery on and left. LSU scored 17 unanswered after that. He is a game-time decision. Makhi Frazier and JT Lindsey have already been leaned on. Trinidad Chambliss is 77 of 110 for 924 yards, and he ran for 112 and two scores against Tulane, Sumrall's old team. Deuce Alexander, kept after the coaching change, caught five passes for 159 and two scores in the opener. The defense has 46 missed tackles, and the secondary gave up 12 plays of 20-plus yards for 340.

Florida has not lost to Ole Miss at home since 2008, and has not beaten a top-five team since Georgia in 2020. The last time a top-five Ole Miss team came here, Florida won 38-10. Baugh is coming off 164 yards and three scores, and he has forced 30 missed tackles, the most in the country. Ole Miss allows 159 rushing yards a game. Duke Clark added a 20-yard score on third and goal at Auburn, where Florida rushed for 243. Vernell Brown is 19, has 246 receiving yards, and says he has never been afraid to speak up. Aaron Philo is the game manager. Jayden Woods, who intercepted Chambliss last November, is the pass rusher. Sumrall said last week's performance will not be enough. Eric Singleton is a game-time decision with an ankle and is trending toward playing.

Where the defense is weaker

  • Ole Miss: They have already allowed points to Louisville and LSU. Philo likes to hold the ball and throw down the field. That is a shot at this secondary, and it is also how he takes hits.
  • Florida: The defense has not been asked to stop Louisville or LSU. Baugh is excellent. The secondary is the side Chambliss can attack. This is not a get-right spot against a soft schedule anymore.

What the scoreboard forces

  • Florida is favored by 3.5 at home. The books have Florida around 31 and Ole Miss around 28. One desk has the game around 51, under 58.5, because these teams have not always combined for 60.
  • If Florida pulls away, Baugh's 8.5 yards a carry is the script and Chambliss has to throw into a building that just beat LSU's offense emotionally.
  • If Ole Miss hangs around, Chambliss's 363 against LSU is the reason, and Philo holds the ball looking downfield.
  • If Ole Miss takes the lead, Philo has to throw. He has completed 73.4 percent. He also holds the ball.

Who that points at

  • Chambliss is the better quarterback on the card, the one who threw for 363 against LSU.
  • If Chambliss is on the six, keep Alexander or Odom with him.
  • If Philo is on the six, keep Brown or Stockton with him.
  • Sit Deuce Knight and Tramell Jones Jr. They are the backups. Do not captain Lacy while he is doubtful. Sit Kelvin Jimenez. He is out.
