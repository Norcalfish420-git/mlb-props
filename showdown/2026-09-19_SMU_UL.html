2) SMU @ Louisville · 3:30 p.m. ET · L&N Stadium · ESPN2

Louisville is about 1.5 points better at home. The total sits at 59.5.

This slate is five out of five stars. This is a one-score ACC opener. The live book is Louisville −1.5 / 59.5. UL on DraftKings is Louisville, not Louisiana. 3:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2. Outdoor.

WEATHER: SMU@LOU — outdoor — 92°F at kick, wind 7 mph — rain chance peaks around 24% in the third quarter. Heat, not a washout.

WHO THEY ARE

  • SMU is ranked No. 16 this year (2-0).
  • Louisville is ranked No. 23 this year (1-1).

WHO MATTERS

These are the names that decide a six tonight — who throws, who runs the ball, who catches it, and who to sit.

SMU

Quarterbacks

  • Kevin Jennings — Starting quarterback. Throws from the pocket. Last week vs UC Davis he threw 16 times for 337, 5 passing scores and had 3 carries for 5, all in the first half. Through two games he is 40-of-52 for 767 yards and 8 passing scores. Last year he threw for 3,641 yards, 26 scores, and 13 interceptions. A jammed right index finger in camp is cleared. SMU goes as he goes.
  • Ty Hawkins — The backup quarterback. Sit him — a backup priced like a starter is still the backup. Last week vs UC Davis, after Jennings sat, he threw 13 times for 104 and 2 passing scores. That was garbage time in a 56–10 game, not a job tonight.

Running backs

They do not have one locked-in workhorse this week. Kendrick Raphael is listed first.

  • Kendrick Raphael — Starting running back. California transfer; last year 232 carries, 943 yards, 13 scores. Week 1 at Florida State he ran 12 times for 75. Last week vs UC Davis he did not get a rushing tape — 1 catch for 23 while Jennings threw the game open. He is still the numbered back.
  • Christian Rhodes — Out. Sit him.

Receiving room

  • Yamir Knight — Starting slot receiver. Camp WR1 with Jennings. Last year 54 catches, 637 yards, 5 scores. The coach talked about a 70–100 catch season in that James Proche mold — underneath separator, not the contested-catch guy on the boundary. Last week vs UC Davis: 3 catches for 62. Week 1 at Florida State: 6 for 49, and he muffed a punt.
  • Jalen Cooper — Starting X receiver. Last week vs UC Davis he had 1 catch for 42. Week 1 at Florida State: 4 for 71 and a 50-yard score. He won the outside job in a crowded camp.
  • Yannick Smith — Starting Z receiver. ECU transfer. Week 1 at Florida State: 5 for 107 and a 79-yard score, his fourth career 100-yard game. Last week vs UC Davis: 1 catch for 44 and a score. Size on the boundary.
  • Jalen Hale — Rotational receiver behind Smith at Z. Last week vs UC Davis: 1 catch for 51. Week 1: 2 for 70. Vertical shots, not Knight-style volume.
  • Randy Pittman Jr. — The backup tight end / H-back, and the one who has actually caught the ball. Florida State transfer. Week 1: 5 for 75 and a 10-yard score. Last week vs UC Davis: 1 catch for 2 and a score. SMU moves him around rather than parking him as a stay-home blocker.
  • Theo Melin Ohrstrom — Starting tight end on the school chart. We do not have last-week receiving tape. Pittman has been the pass-game tight end so far.

Louisville

Quarterbacks

  • Lincoln Kienholz — Starting quarterback. Runs and throws. Last week vs Villanova he threw 21 times for 332, 2 passing scores and had 6 carries for 42. That was a quiet rushing night, not who he is. Through two games: 34-of-50 for 639 yards and 4 passing scores, plus 111 rushing yards and 3 rushing scores, no interceptions. Ohio State transfer; he is the centerpiece of this offense.
  • Davin Wydner — The backup quarterback. Sit him — a backup priced like a starter is still the backup. Last week vs Villanova he threw 4 times for 19 and a score. That is mop-up, not a job tonight.

Running backs

They split the carries. Isaac Brown (13 carries) and Marquise Davis (7 carries). Use one of them, not both. The back who scored: Marquise Davis.

  • Isaac Brown — Starting running back. Expected to play (probable). Through two games he has 29 carries for 97 yards and no rushing scores — 13 for 61 last week vs Villanova. He has also caught 3 balls for 32. The quarterback is a real goal-line runner, so this is not a bell-cow price tag.
  • Marquise Davis — Last week vs Villanova: 7 carries for 86 and a score when the room mixed in. Committee piece, not the numbered first back.
  • Keyjuan Brown — Last week vs Villanova: 1 carry for 12. Real committee, not a ghost. Do not play two Louisville backs.

Receiving room

  • Tre Richardson — Starting slot receiver. Last week vs Villanova: 7 catches for 98 plus a designed run. The staff wants him involved every week. Keep him with Kienholz.
  • Lawayne McCoy — Starting Z receiver. Last week vs Villanova: 3 catches for 102 and a score. One of the 60-plus-yard explosives already this year.
  • TreyShun Hurry — Starting X receiver. Last week vs Villanova: 2 catches for 29. Week 1 at Ole Miss: 4 for 67.
  • Jaleel Skinner — Starting tight end. Last week vs Villanova: 2 catches for 82 and a score. That is 41 yards a catch on the night — the pass-game tight end, not a blocker.
  • Brody Foley — The backup tight end. Last week vs Villanova: 1 catch for 10. Sit him behind Skinner.

Game Script

No. 16 SMU visits No. 23 Louisville at L&N Stadium, 3:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2. Jeff Brohm is at home. The books have Louisville about a field goal better and both teams around 59 points together. It is 92 degrees, wind at 7, and only a slim rain chance in the third quarter — heat, not a washout. Two quarterbacks who have been lighting it up meet in a one-score ACC opener. This is a toss-up, not a three-score stack.

What the writers are saying: Split. The Louisville paper has SMU 42–38 because the Cardinals cannot slow Jennings. Other writers pick Louisville at home by a field goal and like the over. One desk wants to live-bet it rather than trust either side at 1.5. Both offenses grade as explosive; both have also started slow in the first quarter. The over still has friends once they settle in.

SMU wants to throw. Rhett Lashlee’s offense stacked 547 yards on UC Davis after 585 at Florida State, and Jennings just threw five scoring passes — three in the red zone, two from 44 and 52 yards, all in the first half. The worry is the ball: four turnovers in the Florida State escape. Louisville’s defense slowed Ole Miss for a half, then the second half became a shootout. The local paper’s read is that they will struggle to keep Jennings in front of them. Knight is the slot security blanket; Cooper and Smith are the outside shots; Pittman is the tight end who has actually caught scores.

Louisville wants to throw and run with the quarterback. Kienholz has seven total scores and no picks through two games, 300-plus passing yards in both, and he leads the team in rushing. Isaac Brown is the numbered back and is expected to play, but he has 97 yards on 29 carries and has not broken a big game yet — last week they mixed Marquise Davis (7 for 86 and a score) and Keyjuan Brown. Brohm has already manufactured touches for Tre Richardson (7 for 98 last week). Three different receivers have 60-plus-yard plays. SMU’s defense gets after the quarterback (sacks vs Florida State and UC Davis); Kienholz was sacked three times against Ole Miss.

If Louisville leads by two scores, this becomes Kienholz plus the running back, not more Jennings. If it stays one score, both pass trees stay alive. An SMU lead asks Kienholz to throw and run. Captain either quarterback. Keep Richardson with Kienholz. Two SMU receivers, not three. Sit the backup quarterbacks.

Where the defense is weaker

  • SMU: Four turnovers against Florida State is the offensive worry. On defense they have gotten after the quarterback; nobody we read named a hole they cannot cover.
  • Louisville: Explosives already this year. Three different receivers with 60-plus-yard plays. The paper thinks they will struggle to slow Jennings.

What the scoreboard forces

  • A two-score Louisville lead is Kienholz plus Brown, not more Jennings
  • A one-score game keeps both pass trees alive
  • An SMU lead asks Kienholz to throw and run

Who that points at

  • Kienholz or Jennings as captain.
  • Richardson with Kienholz
  • Two SMU receivers, not three
  • Not a backup quarterback priced like a starter.
