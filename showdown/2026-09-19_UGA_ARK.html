Game Script

Georgia is the three-score favorite on the road. Arkansas is home and still the dog — and they are on a short week after a late kick at Utah. Ryan Silverfield has the Arkansas job after Memphis. Kirby Smart's first-teamers have not been asked to play a full SEC road test yet. Gunner Stockton has been efficient — 87.5 percent completions, 133 points in two games, the kind of leap people have been waiting on. Nate Frazier is the Georgia back: 12.7 yards a carry with four scores on just 10 attempts, and the line has been mauling people. Lawson Luckie is the Georgia tight end (DraftKings buckets him as a receiver). Dante Dowdell is out. Several Georgia defensive backs and a linebacker are out. Arkansas tight ends Lassiter and Hendrix are out.

What the writers are saying: Georgia covers and the total stays under. Arkansas just got run off the field at Utah. Georgia's defense has given up almost nothing through two games. One desk has Georgia 49–7. Another is selling high on the Dawgs in their first real road game and taking Arkansas plus three scores — Kirby does not usually run it up in this spread range, and the passing attack may lack elite explosiveness on the outside. This is still a 5–1 Georgia script, not a 3–3.
