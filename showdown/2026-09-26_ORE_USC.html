Game Script

No. 20 Oregon (2-1) at No. 12 USC (4-0). The books have Oregon around 33 and USC around 28. Oregon lost to Oklahoma State 39-31, then beat Portland State 84-0. USC has allowed 25 or more in three of four games, including 35 to Rutgers. Dante Moore's last game was 19 of 23 for 280 yards and four touchdowns. Earlier he threw for 378 against Boise State. Jayden Maiava has 1,173 yards, 12 touchdowns and one interception. His last game was 20 of 32 for 277 and two scores. King Miller's last game was 16 carries for 102 and a touchdown. Evan Stewart's last game was three catches for 72 and a score. Jeremiah McClellan is out with a broken foot. Trent Mosley is out. Jamari Johnson is questionable.

What the writers are saying: The Oregon paper has the Ducks 38-27 and says the game is won up front. Oregon has 10 tackles for loss and five sacks in three games, which is not enough against Jayden Maiava, and the run game has to feature Jordon Davison early the way Rutgers' back went for 122. The USC side has the Trojans 42-38 if the edge cannot set the contain. A broadcast pick is USC 34-31 because the Trojans' line has allowed two sacks in four games, Oregon has been sacked seven times, and Dan Lanning is 43-0 when the other team scores under 28. USC has not scored under 39. The Ducks' own staff is on Oregon 35-31, 31-24, and 39-34, and says Dante Moore needs the run so he is not living on dropbacks. One model still wants the under and lands around 58, with Moore around 266 and Maiava around 268. The number has come in from about a 5.5-point Oregon favorite. Our book is Oregon -3.5 and 61.5.

Oregon lost 39-31 at Oklahoma State, where Moore was 14 of 29 for 191 and was sacked four times, then hung 84 on Portland State. They were 0 for 6 on fourth down in the first two games. Jamari Johnson missed both of those games and is questionable. He is the run blocker and Moore's safety valve. Freshman Kendre Harrison is also banged up. Without Johnson, the offense looked lost in Stillwater. Jeremiah McClellan is out with a broken foot, so Dakorien Moore, Evan Stewart, and Messiah Hampton are the receivers left. Establishing Davison, the Los Angeles back, is how they keep this from becoming a track meet. On the other side, Waymond Jordan's carries are down to about nine a game while he works back from the ankle, and King Miller has been the back. One model has Stewart around seven catches for 78.

USC has scored at least 39 every week and converted 30 of 48 third downs. Maiava leads the country with 1,173 yards and 12 touchdowns, and the line in front of him has allowed two sacks. Last year his completion rate fell from 74 percent at home to 57.5 on the road, and he is home. Rutgers threw for 252 and scored 35, with 21 of those points in the second half, the same leak that showed up against San Jose State and Louisiana. Nine Rutgers receivers caught a ball. Trent Mosley, 13 catches for 255 and four scores, is out with a knee. Kayden Dixon-Wyatt leads the room with 258 yards. King Miller has back-to-back 100-yard games. Lincoln Riley is still looking for his first win over Dan Lanning. USC is 5-14 against ranked teams in his tenure.

Where the defense is weaker

  • USC: Rutgers threw for 252 and scored 35. The run defense also gave up a hundred-yard back. Moore's receivers are a harder ask than that.
  • Oregon: Oklahoma State already showed the leak. Maiava will try to do the same thing. The Portland State score does not erase that tape.

What the scoreboard forces

  • Oregon is favored by 3.5 on the road. The books have Oregon around 33 and USC around 28. Another has the total landing around 58, under 61.5.
  • If Oregon pulls away, Davison and Hill get the carries, and Maiava has to throw against a defense that just lost to Oklahoma State before the Portland State blowout.
  • Rutgers already scored 35 here and threw for 252. If this stays close, Moore and Maiava both have to throw into that.
  • If USC takes the lead, Moore has to throw. That is the captain case.

Who that points at

  • Dante Moore is the road favorite who just threw four touchdowns. Maiava is the home quarterback with 1,173 yards and 12 scores.
  • If Moore is on the six, keep Stewart or Dakorien Moore with him. McClellan is out with a broken foot, so those are the receivers left.
  • If Maiava is on the six, keep Dixon-Wyatt or Hines with him. Mosley is out.
  • Miller gets the USC carries, 16 for 102 last game. Hill is the Oregon back the sheet is paying for points. Davison gets the carries if Oregon sits on a lead.
  • Sit Dylan Raiola and Jonas Williams. They are the backups. Sit McClellan and Mosley. They are out. Johnson is questionable, so he is not the captain.
