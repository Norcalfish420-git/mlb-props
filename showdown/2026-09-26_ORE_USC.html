CFB Showdown — 2026-09-26 · Oregon @ USC

4) Oregon @ USC · 7:30 p.m. ET · Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum · NBC

This slate is five out of five stars. No. 20 Oregon is a small road favorite at No. 12 USC. The live book is Oregon −3.5 / 61.5. 7:30 p.m. ET on NBC. Outdoor. The Coliseum.

  • Oregon -3.5.
  • Total 61.5.
  • Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, night.

Game Script

No. 20 Oregon (2-1) at No. 12 USC (4-0). The books have Oregon around 33 and USC around 28. Oregon lost to Oklahoma State 39-31, then beat Portland State 84-0. USC has allowed 25 or more in three of four games, including 35 to Rutgers. Dante Moore's last game was 19 of 23 for 280 yards and four touchdowns. Earlier he threw for 378 against Boise State. Jayden Maiava has 1,173 yards, 12 touchdowns and one interception. His last game was 20 of 32 for 277 and two scores. King Miller's last game was 16 carries for 102 and a touchdown. Evan Stewart's last game was three catches for 72 and a score. Jeremiah McClellan is out with a broken foot. Trent Mosley is out. Jamari Johnson is questionable.

What the writers are saying: Oregon is the side. The total is the argument. One desk has Oregon 33-29 because Moore's receivers should have a day against a defense Rutgers just moved. The projection model wants the under at 61.5 and lands around 58, with Moore around 266 and two scores and Maiava around 268 and a couple of scores. Another desk still likes the over because USC scores at home and these two put points up. The number has come in from about a 5.5-point Oregon favorite earlier in the week. We play it as a one-score night game.

Oregon wants Moore to throw. Dakorien Moore, Evan Stewart, and Messiah Hampton are the receivers with McClellan out. Jordon Davison is charted first at running back. Dierre Hill Jr. is charted second and the sheet likes his points. Jamari Johnson is the tight end and he is questionable.

USC wants Maiava to throw. He has been as good as any quarterback in the league. Tanook Hines and Kayden Dixon-Wyatt are the outside receivers with Mosley out. King Miller is charted first at running back. Waymond Jordan is charted second. Mark Bowman is the tight end with a posted receptions number.

Where the defense is weaker

  • USC: Rutgers threw for 252 and scored 35. The run defense also gave up a hundred-yard back. Moore's receivers are a harder ask than that.
  • Oregon: Oklahoma State already showed the leak. Maiava will try to do the same thing. The Portland State score does not erase that tape.

What the scoreboard forces

  • Oregon is favored by 3.5 on the road. The books have both teams around 30. This is a one-score game with a high total.
  • If Oregon gets up by two scores, they can run. Davison and Hill get work, and Maiava has to throw.
  • If it stays within a score, both quarterbacks throw the rest of the night. That is what the books expect.
  • If USC takes the lead, Moore has to throw. That is the captain case.

Who that points at

  • Captain Dante Moore or Maiava. Both throw. Moore is the road favorite. Maiava is the home heater.
  • If Moore is on the six, keep Stewart or Dakorien Moore with him.
  • If Maiava is on the six, keep Dixon-Wyatt or Hines with him.
  • Miller is the USC back. Hill is the Oregon back the sheet is paying. Davison is still charted first.
  • Sit Dylan Raiola and Jonas Williams. They are backups. Sit McClellan and Mosley. They are out. Johnson is questionable, so he is not the captain.
