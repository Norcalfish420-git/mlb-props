Game Script

This slate is four out of five stars.

How they want to play

  • Pittsburgh: Pittsburgh throws more than it runs, and the yards have come through the air. In 3 games they have thrown 126 times (42 a game) for 422 yards a game and run it 104 times (35 a game) for 154 yards a game. Mason Heintschel has done most of the throwing.
  • Virginia Tech: Virginia Tech mixes the pass and the run, and the yards have come through the air. In 4 games they have thrown 150 times (38 a game) for 285 yards a game and run it 160 times (40 a game) for 178 yards a game. Ethan Grunkemeyer has done most of the throwing. James Franklin came from Penn State. Jeffrey Overton Jr. is the Hokies run. Marcellous Hawkins Jr. has been the quieter back. Ayden Greene and Montavious Cunningham were expected back.

Where the defense is weaker

  • Pittsburgh: In 3 games opponents have thrown for 184 yards a game against Pittsburgh and run for 26. The yards have come through the air. Those games were Miami (OH), UCF, and Bucknell. Pitt defense has held teams down. Pitt defense has been the better one. Pitt defense has stuffed the run.
  • Virginia Tech: In 4 games opponents have thrown for 186 yards a game against Virginia Tech and run for 83. The yards have come through the air. Those games were VMI, Old Dominion, Maryland, and Boston College. Maryland threw for 359 on the Hokies.

What the scoreboard forces:

  • Pittsburgh plus 3.5, and the under.
  • The score lean is Virginia Tech 27, Pittsburgh 24.

What the writers are saying: Virginia Tech path runs through the quiet Mason Heintschel, not the six-score night against Bucknell. Pitt is on its first road trip. Cortez Harris has five sacks. Take Pittsburgh plus the points. Feed Jeffrey Overton Jr. Overton just broke a long one at Boston College. Justin Terry is out. Que'Sean Brown has been a problem on punt returns.
