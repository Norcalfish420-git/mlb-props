Game Script

This slate is three out of five stars.

No. 13 Alabama (5-2, 3-2 SEC) hosts Texas A&M (4-3, 1-3 SEC) at Bryant-Denny on Saturday, Oct. 24, at 2:30 p.m. CT on ABC. Alabama just lost at home to Georgia, 20-17, and at Tennessee, 26-10. That is the first time the Tide has lost two in a row since 2013. Texas A&M has not won a road game this year. The Aggies scored six points at LSU and six points at Missouri, then beat The Citadel 41-0 at home. They have not won in Tuscaloosa since 2012.

Keelon Russell has been Alabama's quarterback all season. He runs and he throws, and the offense went quiet in those two losses. At Tennessee he completed 19 of 29 passes for 147 yards, threw two interceptions, and ran 11 times. Marcel Reed is the Texas A&M quarterback. The Aggies' offense is the part of this matchup that does not travel, and a rebound from Alabama's offense at home is the path to the Tide covering a game that still may not be a blowout.

Reeder picks Alabama. He says Texas A&M's third-down defense is the reason it can stay closer than the 13.5-point number, and he calls this a bad road spot for the Aggies: Alabama is home after two losses, and A&M has not won in this stadium since 2012.
