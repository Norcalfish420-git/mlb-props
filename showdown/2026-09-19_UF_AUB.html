CFB Showdown — 2026-09-19 · Florida @ Auburn

3) Florida @ Auburn · 7:00 p.m. ET · Jordan-Hare Stadium · ESPN

Florida is about 2.5 points better. The total sits at 53.5. Jordan-Hare, night.

This slate is four out of five stars. Florida is a small road favorite. The live book is Florida −2.5 / 53.5. 7 p.m. ET on ESPN. Outdoor.

WEATHER: FLA@AUB — outdoor — 84°F at kick, wind 4 mph — slight-chance storms. One forecast has rain around kick, then it dries. Not a confirmed washout.

Game Script

Florida is a small road favorite at Jordan-Hare, 7 p.m. ET on ESPN. Two SEC bluebloods putting unblemished records on the line — a litmus test of both rebuilds, and their first meeting since 2011. Jon Sumrall has the Gators after a playoff run at Tulane. Alex Golesh has Auburn — first year after South Florida, and he brought Byrum Brown with him. The books have Florida about a field goal better and both teams around 53 points together. It is 84 degrees, wind at 4, with a slight storm chance at kick that one forecast says dries — not a confirmed washout. This is a one-score SEC opener, not a three-score stack.

What the writers are saying: Split. The market is treating Florida as the known quantity after 118 points in two games, but those were Campbell and Florida Atlantic. One desk takes the Gators on the moneyline as a short road favorite in a loud night stadium. Another takes Auburn to win outright: they already have a quality win over Baylor, they have the better quarterback, and Florida has not picked on someone their own size. We are not locking a three-score Florida stack.

Florida wants to throw and hand it to Baugh. Sumrall is an offensive specialist, and it has shown — 118 points already. Georgia Tech transfer Aaron Philo has been fantastic (517 yards, five scores through two). Jadan Baugh is one of the premier backs in the country: 296 rushing yards (4th), five scores, 10.6 yards a carry. Vernell Brown III is the slot you keep with Philo; Dallas Wilson is the outside shot. Eric Singleton missed the first two games and is a game-time ankle — fade the volume until he is cleared. Kahleil Jackson is a watch. Micah Mays Jr. is depth, not the case.

Auburn wants to throw and run with the quarterback. Golesh’s whole idea is let Byrum Brown do his thing. No team knows that better than Florida — Brown was a one-man wrecking ball at the Swamp when he was still at South Florida. Last week vs Southern Miss he went 29 for 226 with two passing scores and 13 carries for 88 after a sloppy Baylor opener. Mabson and Cobb split the backfield last week (Mabson 15 for 108 and a score; Cobb 12 for 85). Nimrod is the Auburn receiver you keep with Byrum Brown (7 for 52 last week). Cam Coleman is at Texas — sit that name. The worry is a brand-new offensive line from the portal, and Brown’s habit of turning it over.

Where the defense is weaker

  • Florida: New leadership. Brad White came from Kentucky and runs 3-man and 4-man fronts — stop the run, get pressure, cut out explosives. Third-down defense has been ordinary, and this is the first real test against a dual-threat.
  • Xavier Atkins is back (17 tackles for loss led the SEC). The rest of that front seven turned over, and that is how Baugh burns them. Pass defense ranked poorly through two games. Red-zone defense has been leaky.

What the scoreboard forces

  • Florida is only a field goal better on the road, and the books have both teams around 53 points together. This is supposed to stay a one-score game, not a blowout.
  • If Florida gets up by two scores, they run more. Jadan Baugh gets the work, and Byrum Brown throws less.
  • If it stays within a score — which is what the books expect — both starting quarterbacks keep playing the rest of the night.
  • If Auburn takes the lead, Philo has to throw. He does not sit on the ball.

Who that points at

  • Captain a starting quarterback or Baugh. Byrum Brown is the home pick because he throws and runs. Baugh is the other captain if Florida’s run game keeps rolling.
  • If Philo is on the six, keep Vernell Brown III with him. That is who Florida wants the ball.
  • If Byrum Brown is on the six, keep Chas Nimrod with him.
  • Sit the backup quarterbacks. They only play if a starter is out, but DraftKings prices them like they might start. Sit Cam Coleman. Do not build the six around Micah Mays. Do not play Singleton until he is cleared.

WHO MATTERS

These are the names that decide a six tonight — who throws, who runs the ball, who catches it, and who to sit. Role and camp first. Last week is the tape, not the whole bio.

Florida

Quarterbacks

  • Aaron Philo — The engine. Pocket passer. Georgia Tech transfer. Fantastic through two — 517 yards and five scores — but this is the first road SEC test. Sumrall said he makes better checks than Tramell Jones, and Florida goes as he goes on the road. The week before vs Florida Atlantic: 21 for 275, 3 passing scores, 6 carries for 20 and a rushing score. Last week vs Campbell he threw 21 times for 242, 2 passing scores, and ran one in (3 carries, 13 yards).
  • Tramell Jones Jr. — The backup. Sit him — a backup priced like a starter is still the backup.

Running backs

Baugh is the numbered back. Use him, not the committee.

  • Jadan Baugh — The Florida back. One of the premier halfbacks in the country — 296 yards (4th), five scores, 10.6 a carry. Volume guy, not a change-of-pace. The week before vs Florida Atlantic: 14 for 160 and 3 scores. Last week vs Campbell: 14 carries, 136 yards, 2 rushing scores. That is the work.
  • Duke Clark — Next on the list behind Baugh. Last week was quiet. Not a captain.
  • Evan Pryor — Committee. Not a captain.
  • London Montgomery — Committee. Not a captain.

Receiving room

  • Dallas Wilson — The outside shot. That is who Philo hit for the chunk last week. Last week vs Campbell: 5 catches for 104 and a score.
  • Vernell Brown III — The slot. The security blanket you keep with Philo. The week before vs Florida Atlantic he was the volume (6 for 117 and a score). Last week vs Campbell was quieter (3 for 43 and a score) but that is still the job.
  • TJ Abrams — The other outside receiver. Named as a Singleton fill with Stockton if Singleton sits. Last week vs Campbell: 3 for 33.
  • Luke Harpring — The tight end in the pass game, not a stay-home blocker. Last week vs Campbell: 3 for 33.
  • Eric Singleton Jr. — Receiver with a game-time ankle vs Auburn. Fade the volume until he is cleared.
  • Bailey Stockton — Named as the Singleton fill with Abrams. Not the volume — Brown and Wilson are. Last week vs Campbell: 4 for 41.
  • Micah Mays Jr. — Depth. Last week vs Campbell: 1 catch for 1. If Singleton sits, desks name Stockton and Abrams first. Do not build the six around him.
  • Kahleil Jackson — Thursday’s SEC report did not list him out. Prior ESPN Out stamp is stale. Watch, not a sit.

Auburn

Quarterbacks

  • Byrum Brown — The engine of this offense. Throws and runs. Golesh brought him from South Florida, where he was a one-man wrecking ball — Florida already lost to him at the Swamp. If you make him captain, his rushing yards count extra. Last week vs Southern Miss: 29 for 226, 2 passing scores, 13 carries for 88.
  • Tristan Ti'a — The backup. Sit him. Last week vs Southern Miss was mop-up (4 throws, 39 yards).

Running backs

They split the carries last week. Use one, not both. The back who scored: Omar Mabson II.

  • Omar Mabson II — The back who scored. The projection sheet treated him as the lead even without a rushing-yards number — they think the job is real. Keep him in the hunt, not a captain over Byrum Brown. Last week vs Southern Miss: 15 for 108 and a rushing score.
  • Jeremiah Cobb — Auburn listed him first at running back. Last week they still split it (12 for 85 and 1 catch for 11). Real committee, not a ghost. Do not play two Auburn backs.
  • Bryson Washington — Change-of-pace, not a second Mabson. Last week vs Southern Miss: 7 for 44.
  • Tae Meadows — Extra committee. Last week vs Southern Miss: 3 for 18.

Receiving room

  • Jeremiah Koger — The outside shot. Not the volume guy — that is Nimrod. Last week vs Southern Miss: 2 for 57.
  • Chas Nimrod — The tight end who lines up wide. That is who you keep with Byrum Brown. Last week vs Southern Miss: 7 for 52.
  • Keshaun Singleton — The other outside receiver. Last week vs Southern Miss: 3 for 42.
  • Jake Johnson — The tight end. Quieter than Nimrod in the pass game. Last week vs Baylor: 3 for 20.
  • DeShawn Spencer — True freshman slot. Best offensive player at Auburn’s spring scrimmage (they call it A-Day). In camp he practiced with the starters as the inside receiver. Last week vs Southern Miss: 3 for 24 and a score.
  • Jonathan Echols — Depth who still found the end zone. Last week vs Southern Miss: 1 catch for 22 and a score.
  • Cam Coleman — He is at Texas. Sit any Auburn row under that name.
