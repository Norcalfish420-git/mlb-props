5) Minnesota @ Washington · 11 p.m. ET · Husky Stadium · FOX

This slate is three out of five stars. Minnesota is a two-score road dog at Washington. The live book is Washington −10.5 / 46.5. 11 p.m. ET on FOX. Outdoor. Husky Stadium. Neither team is ranked. Minnesota is 2-1. Washington is 3-0.

Game Script

The books have Washington around 28 and Minnesota around 18. That is the lowest total on this Saturday Showdown slate. Washington has scored about 20 points a game against FBS opponents, and those opponents were Washington State and Utah State. They led Washington State 10-0 at half and Utah State 10-7 at half. Minnesota likes to slow the game down. Drake Lindsey has 747 yards, four touchdowns and one interception. He is a pocket passer. He has been pressured 17 times and sacked twice. When he is hurried he has completed five of 15 for 69 yards. Demond Williams Jr. is the Washington quarterback. The DraftKings report has Jayden Limar, Jordan Washington, and Quaid Carr out at running back, so Brian Bonner Jr. is the back who is left. Dezmen Roebuck is the receiver people keep naming. Darius Taylor is the Minnesota back and he is a game-time decision.

What the writers are saying: The low total is the card. A Washington preview has the Huskies 27-20 and says Drake Lindsey is a pocket passer with a big arm who can throw the sideline on a rope, so the stress is on the secondary if the rush does not get there. He has scrambled on zero of 17 pressures and holds the ball 3.25 seconds when hurried. On the year he is 5 of 15 for 69 under pressure. Against Mississippi State, which brought pressure, he was 3 of 8 for 30, and Minnesota lost 38-13. The Husky site is split: one pick is Washington 27-20, one is Minnesota 20-10 because the Huskies cannot run, and one is 27-24. The average of those three lands in a tie at 21. The local daily has Washington 24-17. Washington's defensive coordinator said the penalties reek of undiscipline: 14 flags for 181 yards, nine personal fouls, eight of them on defenders. Our book is Washington −10.5 and 46.5. We are not building a shootout.

Washington has not covered a number. They were favored by 23.5, 28.5, and 42.5 and failed all three. They led Washington State 10-0 and Utah State 10-7 at half. Demond Williams is the leading rusher, too, with 150 yards on the ground. The top three backs are out, Quaid Carr, Jayden Limar, and Jordan Washington, so freshman Brian Bonner, Ansu Sanoe, and sixth-year Trey Cooley are what is left. Center Landen Hatchett is expected back. Left tackle Kodi Greene and left guard John Mills are 18. Christian Moss lasted one play in the opener and is out for months. Dezmen Roebuck caught nine of nine targets against the FCS team. Rashid Williams and Baron Naone are the other targets, with screens, because the run game is not there. Emmanuel Karnley is questionable with a soft-tissue injury, so freshman Jeron Jones may be the outside corner.

Minnesota lost 38-13 at Mississippi State, the only Power conference team they have seen. The run is the offense, and Darius Taylor's health is why it has stalled. Taylor is probable. Against Mississippi State he lasted 16 snaps and six carries for 15 yards. When he gets more than 10 carries, Minnesota is 17-4. When he does not, they are 7-14. He is not a captain until he is active. Center Ashton Beers was ruled out Friday. Left guard Greg Johnson is the All-American the Husky front still has to handle. Lindsey does not scramble. Anthony Smith had 12.5 sacks last year and has been quiet, which is the matchup for Washington's young left side. Javon Tracy has 240 receiving yards if the throw has to come out quick. TJ Thomas is the back if Taylor is limited.

Where the defense is weaker

  • Washington: The offense is the problem, not a famous hole in the defense. They get tackles for loss. Lindsey does not run away from pressure. If the front gets home, he has been inaccurate. If it does not, he can throw the sideline.
  • Minnesota: They are the dog in a low total. Washington can still grind a lead if Bonner runs. This is not a defense we are stacking touchdowns against.

What the scoreboard forces

  • Washington is favored by 10.5 at home. The books have Minnesota around 18 and Washington around 28. They have scored about 20 a game against FBS teams.
  • If Washington pulls away, Bonner is the back left after three teammates were ruled out, and Lindsey has to throw from behind. Under pressure he is five of 15 for 69.
  • If Minnesota hangs close, Lindsey's pocket throws are the plus-the-points ticket. One scoreboard lands around Washington 27, Minnesota 20.
  • A fast Washington start is not what the first halves have looked like. A slow start keeps the total down and keeps the dog alive.

Who that points at

  • Captain Williams or Bonner if you want the favorite. Lindsey is the dog quarterback if you think this stays inside the number.
  • If Williams is on the six, keep Roebuck with him.
  • If Lindsey is on the six, keep a Minnesota receiver with him. Taylor only if he is active.
  • Sit the backup quarterbacks. Sit Limar, Jordan Washington, and Quaid Carr. They are out. Taylor is game-time, not a captain. Sit the kickers.
