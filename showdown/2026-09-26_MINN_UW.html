5) Minnesota @ Washington · 11 p.m. ET · Husky Stadium · FOX

This slate is three out of five stars. Minnesota is a two-score road dog at Washington. The live book is Washington −10.5 / 46.5. 11 p.m. ET on FOX. Outdoor. Husky Stadium. Neither team is ranked. Minnesota is 2-1. Washington is 3-0.

Game Script

The books have Washington around 28 and Minnesota around 18. That is the lowest total on this Saturday Showdown slate. Washington has scored about 20 points a game against FBS opponents, and those opponents were Washington State and Utah State. They led Washington State 10-0 at half and Utah State 10-7 at half. Minnesota likes to slow the game down. Drake Lindsey has 747 yards, four touchdowns and one interception. He is a pocket passer. He has been pressured 17 times and sacked twice. When he is hurried he has completed five of 15 for 69 yards. Demond Williams Jr. is the Washington quarterback. The DraftKings report has Jayden Limar, Jordan Washington, and Quaid Carr out at running back. Brian Bonner Jr. is the back who is charted first and is not on that out list. Dezmen Roebuck is the receiver people keep naming. Darius Taylor is the Minnesota back and he is a game-time decision.

What the writers are saying: The dog and the under. One desk takes Minnesota plus the points and the under, because Washington has not played like a two-score favorite and neither offense creates many big plays. A local defensive quote says Lindsey can throw the sideline rope, so the stress is on the Washington secondary if they do not get there fast. Another scoreboard lands around Washington 27, Minnesota 20. The public number is a touchdown-and-a-half. Our book is Washington −10.5 and 46.5. We are not building a shootout.

Washington wants Williams to throw and Bonner to run, because the other backs on the report are out. Roebuck is the receiver. Rashid Williams and Chris Lawson are the other outside options. Decker DeGraaf is the tight end.

Minnesota wants Lindsey to throw from the pocket and Taylor to run if he plays. Jalen Smith, Noah Jennings, and Javon Tracy are the receivers. Pierce Walsh is the tight end. TJ Thomas is the second back if Taylor sits.

Where the defense is weaker

  • Washington: The offense is the problem, not a famous hole in the defense. They get tackles for loss. Lindsey does not run away from pressure. If the front gets home, he has been inaccurate. If it does not, he can throw the sideline.
  • Minnesota: They are the dog in a low total. Washington can still grind a lead if Bonner runs. This is not a defense we are stacking touchdowns against.

What the scoreboard forces

  • Washington is favored by 10.5 at home. The books have Minnesota around 18. Points are supposed to be hard.
  • If Washington gets up by two scores, they can sit on it. Bonner and the defense get the work. Lindsey has to throw from behind into pressure.
  • If Minnesota hangs within a score, Lindsey keeps throwing and the plus-the-points ticket lives. That is the writer script.
  • A fast Washington start is not what the first halves have looked like. A slow start keeps the total down and keeps the dog alive.

Who that points at

  • Captain Williams or Bonner if you want the favorite. Lindsey is the dog quarterback if you think this stays inside the number.
  • If Williams is on the six, keep Roebuck with him.
  • If Lindsey is on the six, keep a Minnesota receiver with him. Taylor only if he is active.
  • Sit the backup quarterbacks. Sit Limar, Jordan Washington, and Quaid Carr. They are out. Taylor is game-time, not a captain. Sit the kickers.
