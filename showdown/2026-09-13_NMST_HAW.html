CFB Showdown — 2026-09-13 · NMST @ Hawai'i

1) NMST @ Hawai'i · 6 p.m. HT · Ching Complex

Hawaii is about 7.5 points better. The total sits at 50.5. Clarence T.C. Ching Complex, 6 p.m. HT / 11:59 p.m. ET on MW+. Outdoor. National Weather Service Honolulu (3:35 p.m. HT Saturday): mostly clear tonight, low around 76, east wind 10–15 mph, easing after midnight. That is not a pass kill.

This slate is three out of five stars. One-score home favorite. The live book is Hawaii −7.5 / 50.5. Late night on the island, and Hawaii is 0–2 with the star catcher still out.

These are the names that decide a six tonight — who runs the ball, who catches it, and who to sit.

Hawaii opened the year with playoff talk and is 0–2 after Stanford 37–27 and UNLV 21–6. New Mexico State is 1–1: lost at Florida State 34–17, then beat Mercyhurst 51–14. Timmy Chang said Alejado is ready after a calf rest Tuesday. Tony Sanchez’s group already covered at Florida State.

What the writers are saying: Do not trust Hawaii as a 7.5-point favorite. Several previews take New Mexico State +7.5. One score they like is New Mexico State 31, Hawaii 27. One model still has Hawaii winning and covering. The total is split — Over from Hedden’s yards, Under from Hawaii’s two slow weeks.

GAME FLOW

How this game plays

  • One-score favorite at home. Nobody sits on a huge lead unless Hawaii actually starts scoring
  • The books have both teams scoring about 50.5 together
  • Hawaii scored 20 points in about eight minutes at Stanford and 13 points in the other 112 minutes of the season

How they want to play

  • Hawaii offense: Run-and-shoot. Alejado throws. Rice is the back. The line gave up five sacks vs UNLV
  • Hawaii defense: UNLV ran for 250. Nobody we read named a hole from that game
  • New Mexico State offense: Hedden throws it downfield. Jones runs when it is there. They piled up 553 yards vs Mercyhurst and 47 rush yards at Florida State
  • New Mexico State defense: Six returning starters. They made Florida State’s quarterback work. Nick Session already has two interceptions

Where the defense is weaker

  • Hawaii: Run defense got mashed by UNLV. Pass rush on Alejado is the other story
  • New Mexico State: Allowed 40-yard shots last year. Hedden’s own tape is downfield, not a designed runner
  • East wind 10–15 is not a sit

What the scoreboard forces

  • A two-score Hawaii lead is the favorite closeout — Alejado can sit, Rice can grind
  • A one-score game keeps both quarterbacks throwing
  • A two-score New Mexico State lead asks Alejado to throw more than last week’s 41 attempts

Who that points at

  • Alejado as Hawaii’s passer so Uiliata and Harris still count. Put him in a normal slot, not captain, if you captain a Hawaii catcher
  • Hedden as New Mexico State’s passer so King still counts
  • Rice as the Hawaii back (not Barfield)
  • Jones as the New Mexico State back (not a second Aggie back)
  • Ashlock stays off
