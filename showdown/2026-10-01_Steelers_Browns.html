The game

Both teams are 2-1. Pittsburgh is a small road favorite in Cleveland. The total is the lowest on the Week 4 board, so this projects as a field-goal game, not a shootout.

The Steelers beat the Bengals 30-27. Aaron Rodgers was 19 of 34 for 292 yards and three touchdowns. Jaylen Warren ran 17 times for 127 yards and caught three passes for 49. An 87-yard run was called back. Rico Dowdle is inactive again, so Warren is the back.

The Browns beat the Panthers 21-18. Deshaun Watson was 16 of 30 for 144 yards and two touchdowns, and he ran 11 times for 45 yards. Both scores went to Harold Fannin Jr. Center Elgton Jenkins and guard Teven Jenkins are out, and so is receiver Tylan Wallace.

Huntington Bank Field is outdoors. The forecast at kick is about 77 degrees with a south wind around 9 mph and almost no rain. A shower is more likely late than on the opening kick. A second read had the wind at 17 mph and the rain holding off until after the game. Neither reading is strong enough to take the pass game off the card.

Why these six

Jaylen Warren. Best ETR value on the slate at +2.6, and Rico Dowdle is inactive. In a 38.5 he is the captain, not a quarterback who has to throw all night. He ran 17 times for 127 yards and caught three passes for 49 against the Bengals.

Deshaun Watson. The only Browns skill player ETR had ahead of his salary (+0.5). He also ran 11 times for 45 last week. Cleveland’s center and right guard are out, so the runs matter.

Quinshon Judkins. ETR had him 0.3 behind his salary, almost even. He is the Browns back in a low total. He ran for 70 yards against Carolina. He is here because the total is 38.5, not because the sheet loved him.

Harold Fannin Jr. ETR had him 1.4 behind his salary. He is on the card because Watson is on the card. He caught both touchdowns last week, seven for 51. The other Browns receivers were further behind their salaries.

Steelers. Even on the ETR sheet (0.0). A 38.5 puts the defense on the six instead of a second quarterback.

Browns. Best Browns value on the sheet at +0.7, and the cheaper defense. Cleveland just won 21-18.

Left off

Aaron Rodgers is 0.3 behind his salary. A 38.5 does not need his passing line. Roman Wilson is 2.8 behind. Michael Pittman Jr. is 3.9 behind. Jerry Jeudy is 4.9 behind, and he was not targeted on 36 snaps.

DK Metcalf is only 0.1 ahead. He scored from 6 yards last week. At $9,000 he is a passing play, and he would need Rodgers on the card with him. That pair does not fit a 38.5 next to Warren.

Chris Boswell and Andre Szmyt both start, and both are in play in this total. Szmyt made kicks from 48 and 45. Boswell’s kicks against Cincinnati were from 33 and 36. Neither kicker is on the six. The two defenses took those spots.
