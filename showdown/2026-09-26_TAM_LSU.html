CFB Showdown — 2026-09-26 · Texas A&M @ LSU

3) Texas A&M @ LSU · 7:30 p.m. ET · Tiger Stadium · ABC

This slate is four out of five stars. No. 23 Texas A&M visits No. 10 LSU at night. The live book is LSU −8.5 / 51.5. 7:30 p.m. ET on ABC. Outdoor. Death Valley. Lane Kiffin is in his first year at LSU after leaving Ole Miss.

  • LSU -8.5.
  • Total 51.5.

Game Script

No. 23 Texas A&M (2-1) at No. 10 LSU (2-1). Lane Kiffin brought the Ole Miss offense to Baton Rouge. The books have LSU around 30 and Texas A&M around 22. That is more than a field goal and less than a laughable number. LSU lost at Ole Miss 32-24. Texas A&M lost at home to Kentucky 31-21. Sam Leavitt's year line is 732 yards, three touchdowns, five interceptions, 61.3 percent. Last week he was 16 of 27 for 158, one touchdown and one interception, and he ran 17 times for 63 and a score. Marcel Reed's year line is 632 yards, six touchdowns, three interceptions, 59.6 percent. Last week he was 26 of 49 for 236, one touchdown and two interceptions, and he ran 10 times for 80 and a score. Dilin Jones ran 23 times for 106 and a score against Ole Miss. Rueben Owens ran 18 times for 74 against Kentucky. Mario Craver caught 11 passes for 119.

What the writers are saying: Split, and the split is the card. One desk takes Texas A&M plus the points because the number moved a long way from an offseason look that had LSU favored by a field goal, and because both quarterbacks have been shaky. That desk also wants a slower game. The projection model wants the over at 51.5, around 53 points, because this series has been a track meet and LSU's recent games have cleared it. The local LSU desk still picks LSU, something like 31-21, and says Reed has not been the same runner the last two weeks. We keep both quarterbacks. We do not pretend this is a 40-point LSU night.

LSU wants to throw and run with Leavitt. Lane Kiffin's offense still needs the quarterback to settle. Trey'Dez Green is the tight end who actually catches it. Last week he had four grabs for 60. Eugene Wilson III and Winston Watkins Jr. are the receivers with posted numbers. Dilin Jones is the back. He was quiet earlier, then he showed up against Ole Miss.

Texas A&M wants Reed to throw and run. He has dogged LSU with his legs before. The last two games have not looked like that. Mario Craver is the receiver. Isaiah Horton and Ashton Bethel-Roman are the other outside options. Rueben Owens is the back. Jamarion Morrow is charted second and he is doubtful.

Where the defense is weaker

  • Texas A&M: No team in this league has fewer sacks or tackles for loss through three games. Leavitt can settle in if that front does not get home. They also just lost at home to Kentucky.
  • LSU: Leavitt has five interceptions already. Ole Miss moved the ball on them last week. The front has been the better unit. The passing game is the leak if he is late with the ball.

What the scoreboard forces

  • LSU is favored by 8.5 at home. The books have LSU around 30 and Texas A&M around 22. Someone can cover without a shootout.
  • If LSU gets up by two scores, Jones and the defense get the work. Reed has to throw and run from behind.
  • If Texas A&M hangs within a score, both quarterbacks keep playing the whole night. That is the plus-the-points script.
  • A slow start helps the under and helps the dog. A track meet helps the over and asks Leavitt to throw more.

Who that points at

  • Captain Jones or Leavitt if you want the home script. Reed is the other captain if you think the dog stays close, because he runs.
  • If Leavitt is on the six, keep Wilson or Watkins with him. Green is the tight end when the salary fits.
  • If Reed is on the six, keep Craver with him, and Owens on the ground.
  • Sit Brady Hart, Husan Longstreet, and Landen Clark. They are backups. Sit Morrow while he is doubtful. Sit the kickers.
